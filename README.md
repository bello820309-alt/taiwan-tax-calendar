# Taiwan Tax Calendar 🇹🇼

An open-source project to organize Taiwan tax filing deadlines, official tax references, and update history in one searchable calendar.

台灣稅務行事曆是一個開源專案，目標是整理稅務申報期限、官方資料來源與更新紀錄，協助使用者更容易查找應辦事項。

## Project Goals

- Organize recurring and annual Taiwan tax filing deadlines.
- Link each entry to an official government source.
- Record the source publication date and the date it was last verified.
- Track corrections and changes to filing deadlines.
- Make tax calendar data easy to search, reuse, and contribute to.

## Data Quality Standards

- Prefer official sources published by Taiwan government agencies.
- Do not publish a filing deadline as verified unless its source has been checked.
- Record the applicable tax year, eligible taxpayer or entity, filing period, source URL, and verification date whenever available.
- Distinguish confirmed information from information that still requires review.
- Review deadline changes and relevant government announcements before updating entries.

Current Status

The project now includes an initial Taiwan business tax filing deadline record in "data/tax_deadlines.csv".

The initial record covers the general business tax filing cycle and links to an official Ministry of Finance source. Additional tax deadlines will be added after their applicable rules and official references have been reviewed.

Automated calendar features have not yet been implemented.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Please include official source links and explain any changes to dates or tax requirements. Corrections should be supported by authoritative references.

## Disclaimer

This project is for information organization and general reference only. It is not legal, tax, or accounting advice. Filing obligations and deadlines may vary depending on the taxpayer's circumstances and applicable rules. Always confirm requirements with the relevant government authority or a qualified professional.
